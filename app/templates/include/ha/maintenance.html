Run drain checks, have another administrator approve the results, then drain the node. After maintenance, run return checks, obtain a new approval and return the node. Checks and approvals are available here; Change Center keeps the shared approval record and history.

The approver must be an enabled administrator in this group with HA access. The plan author cannot approve their own plan. A new check invalidates the previous approval. This workflow does not power off, reboot or patch a host.

Capacity is an operator assertion backed by the evidence you provide; a scheduler simulation does not establish real application throughput. Existing TCP sessions can delay the handover. Keepalived VIP movement can interrupt connections.
